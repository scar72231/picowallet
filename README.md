# 💰 picowallet - Your Secure Crypto in Your Pocket

## 🚀 What Is picowallet?

picowallet is a **hardware wallet** that keeps your digital money safe. Think of it as a super-secure vault for your cryptocurrency that fits in the palm of your hand. It's built from three simple Amazon parts, requires **no soldering**, and is designed for people who want peace of mind without dealing with complicated technology.

This wallet is perfect for anyone who wants to store stablecoins (digital money that doesn't bounce around in value) safely offline, away from hackers and online threats.

## 🔐 Why Do You Need a Hardware Wallet?

- **Protection from hackers:** Your money stays offline, so online thieves can't touch it
- **Full control:** Only you have the keys to your funds
- **Easy backups:** Simple to recover if you lose your device
- **Peace of mind:** Sleep better knowing your savings are locked away safely

## 🎯 What's Inside the Box

The picowallet comes to life using just three components:

1. **Raspberry Pi Pico 2 W** – The brain of the operation
2. **LCD Screen** – Shows your balance and confirms transactions
3. **ATECC608 Security Chip** – Military-grade encryption for your keys

No soldering iron needed. No engineering degree required. Just three parts and ten minutes of your time.

## 📦 System Requirements

Before you start, make sure you have:

- **A Windows computer** (Windows 10 or 11 recommended)
- **A USB port** (standard size works fine)
- **About 50 MB of free space** on your hard drive
- **An internet connection** to download the setup file
- **The three Amazon parts** (Pico 2 W, LCD, ATECC608)

## 🔧 Connecting Your Pico 2 W Hardware (Easy Guide)

1. **Assemble your three parts** (they click together with no tools)
2. **Plug the Pico into your computer** using the USB cable
3. **Open picowallet** – it should say "Device Found"
4. **Create a new wallet** – the app will generate a secret recovery phrase
5. **Write down your recovery phrase** on paper and store it somewhere safe
6. **Done!** Your hardware wallet is ready to use
